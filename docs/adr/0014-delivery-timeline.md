# ADR-0014: Delivery timeline: go-live at end of week 18

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-11
- Decision ID: D14

## Context

The original 14-week estimate predates the bilingual requirement (ADR-0002) and operational features (ADR-0009).

## Decision

Plan four milestones: M0 foundations (weeks 1–2), M1 bilingual catalogue and storefront (3–8), M2 checkout, payments and orders (9–13), M3 completion, hardening and go-live (14–18), then hypercare (19–20). Reviews and wishlist stay in MVP.

## Consequences

- A milestone starts only when the previous one meets its exit criteria.
- Business work (contracts, legal, content) runs in parallel from week 1.
- Phase 2 follows over three months after go-live.
